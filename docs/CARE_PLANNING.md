# Profile care and calendar planning

All new fields are optional on the existing version-1 account document. Older records and backups continue to load. Microchip details remain private account data; the app does not query an external chip registry.

## Approximate food and water

The profile labels the output **approx.** and explains that it is a rough planning guide, not a scientific assessment or feeding prescription. Convert pounds to kilograms first. The starting food-energy calculation is `70 × kg^0.75`, multiplied by an approximate age/activity factor (young puppy 3, older puppy 2, adult low activity 1.3, moderate 1.6, high 1.8; older moderate-activity dogs 1.4). These simple factors cannot establish an individual's needs. No food amount is offered for puppies under eight weeks.

Food grams require the actual food energy density. The explicitly displayed default is 350 kcal/100 g; the owner can replace it using the food label. Wet food often differs substantially. Treats count toward the allowance. Weight, body condition, growth, health and veterinary advice take priority.

The water guide starts at approximately 50–60 ml/kg/day, with broad planning adjustments for young/high-activity dogs. It includes water already in food. It is never a drinking-water limit: fresh drinking water stays freely available and heat/exercise/illness can change needs. Very young puppies require an individual veterinary plan.

## Approving plans

Feeding first asks for meal times; bathing asks for a start date/time and check-in interval; play asks for a daily goal of 20–60 minutes and a preferred time. The next screen offers a manual appointment or a one-year repeat. A separate review displays times, cadence, start/end dates and replacement of any previous managed plan. Only the final confirmation adds series to the account.

A feeding plan stores one daily series per chosen meal time. A play plan stores one daily series; a bathing plan stores a weekly series with an interval. Each has an explicit end after one calendar year, avoiding hundreds of duplicated database entries. Replacing a managed plan keeps unrelated appointments and actual activity records. Manual opens one editable, non-repeating appointment and still requires Save; additional meal appointments can be added in Calendar.

Bathing defaults to an editable six-week **coat check-in**, not compulsory bathing. Coat, skin and medical needs determine whether a bath is needed. Recurrences follow the browser's local wall-clock time, including daylight-saving changes; the current time zone is displayed during approval.

## Activity, charts and badges

Calendar offers Mark play done for today's or past play occurrences. The owner enters actual completed minutes before saving. Undo removes that occurrence's completion. Profile also accepts manual logs and allows removing mistaken entries. Planned minutes never count as completed activity, and owners should avoid logging a scheduled session again manually.

Week and month charts total by day; year charts total by month. Previous/next controls browse earlier periods; an accessible data table supplies each value. Records remain separate for each dog.

Training badges require all lesson practice records in a topic to be complete. Play badges use 500, 1,000, 2,500 and 5,000 recorded minutes. Active-day badges use 30, 60, 90 and 180 distinct dates with recorded play; labels explain that days need not be consecutive. These are recorded-activity milestones, not fitness or training certifications.

## Birthdays

Adding a birthday to a profile automatically creates one yearly Birthday series. Profile edits update that same series ID; clearing the date removes it. The calendar directs birthday changes back to Edit profile. February 29 birthdays appear on February 28 in non-leap years and return to February 29 in leap years. Age calculations use the date of birth when supplied.
