# Mellowoof brand guide

**Mellowoof — Little steps. Happier tails.** The supplied heart-paw logo kit provides the forest-green symbol, rounded vector lettering and sage tagline. The artwork lives in `public/brand/` and retains its original proportions and colours. The full stacked logo is used on sign-in, onboarding and the sidebar; compact mobile branding and small decorative placements use the standalone mark. Nunito remains the interface typeface. Leave generous whitespace around the logo.

Use dark forest text. Pastels are surfaces, not small text on white. Nunito body text uses comfortable line height; headings are 28–38px. Buttons/forms need generous touch targets and visible focus outlines.

The supplied browser favicon, SVG app icon, 192/512px app icons and 180px Apple touch icon are served from their platform paths in `public/`. The PNG app icons have cream backgrounds and standard `any` purpose in the web manifest. The logo kit does not include a dedicated maskable export. Brand images have a Mellowoof accessible name where needed; decorative copies inside already named controls use empty alt text.

Cards use 16–24px rounding, fine borders and subtle shadows. Mobile navigation has five destinations. Dialogs trap focus and support Escape. Motion is short and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Original SVG dog illustrations use soft geometry, muted coats and green collars. Eight avatars include floppy/pointed ears, mixed coats, dark fur and speckles. Worried, resting and happy faces communicate gently. Avoid imagery implying a dog is naughty or broken.

Voice is warm, concise and concrete. “Your pup is learning. So are you.” “That was a tricky one. Let’s make the next step easier.” Never explain fear as dominance, stubbornness, manipulation or badness. Progress is encouragement, not a competition or guarantee of cure.
